Welcome to your Sport Shop!

You have €10.00 to start. Run your shop for 14 days and try to make as much money as you can!

  • 💰 Buy items cheap — sell them for more!
  • 🌤 Check the weather — it changes what sells!
  • 🎯 Set smart prices to get lots of customers!
📦

Stock Your Shop Each Morning

Every morning, choose what to put in your shop. Each item costs money to buy. You sell it for more — that extra money is your profit!

The green bar shows how much profit you could make. The more you stock, the more you could earn!

🌤

Weather Drives Demand

The weather changes every day — and so do your customers! Sunny days mean people want surfboards and swimwear. Windy days bring kite and sailing fans. Rainy days? Hikers show up!

Look at the demand bar on each item. The fuller it is, the more people want it today!

💸

Set Your Selling Price

You decide what to charge for each item. Too expensive and customers leave. Too cheap and you don't make enough profit. Try to find the perfect price!

Watch the satisfaction bar in the simulation — it tells you if your prices are working!

📊

Watch the Simulation

After stocking up, watch customers browse and buy. Track your sales in real time. Customers will leave if items are too pricey, taste is wrong, or you run out of stock.

🦹

Watch Out!

Occasionally a shoplifter 🥷 will sneak in and walk off with 50% of your day's revenue. Keep an eye on your simulation!

Good luck — the town is counting on you to keep the sport shop open!
